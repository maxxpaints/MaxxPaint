FAQs

Common questions

Where is Maxx Paints manufactured?

Maxx Paints products, sold under the Magpa brand, are manufactured in Bihar, India, covering emulsion, distemper, primer, cement paint, wall putty and limewash categories.

How do I become a Maxx Paints dealer or distributor?

Apply from the Become Sellers page, or call +91 93345 10370 / +91 95725 28467. Our team responds within 24-48 hours with area availability and dealer terms.

What is the coverage of Maxx Paints emulsion per litre?

Wall Maxx Emulsion typically covers 120-140 sq. ft. per litre per coat on a properly primed wall, depending on surface porosity and application method. Use our paint calculator for an exact estimate.

Does Maxx Paints offer a shade card / colour catalogue?

Yes — an online shade card is available covering the full range of interior and exterior shades across Wall Maxx Emulsion, distemper and stainers.
